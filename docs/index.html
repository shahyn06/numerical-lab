    ERROR ANALYSIS

    Error as h shrinks

    Truncation error

    Truncation error is the price of replacing the curve by a straight line. Forward and Backward errors shrink in proportion to h — order O(h). Central error shrinks in proportion to h² — order O(h²) — because its symmetric points cancel the leading error term.

    Round-off error

    Round-off error grows when h becomes extremely small: f(a + h) and f(a) are then nearly equal, and subtracting them cancels most of their significant digits. The chart shows both regimes — a steady fall, then a noisy rise at the far left.

    absolute error = |f′ exact(a) − f′ numerical(a)|
